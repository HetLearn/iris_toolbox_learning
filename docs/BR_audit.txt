1. METHOD
---------
Behaviour metrics (numbers in the appendix):
- Turning points: direction changes of each forecast path 2022Q3-2026Q2 (16 quarters), moves
  under 0.01 pp ignored, for inflation, the policy rate, output and consumption growth (all %
  annualised). A second count keeps only reversals of 0.5 pp or more.
- Range: min and max of each path over the 16 quarters.
- Inflation near steady state: pinf at 2026Q2 against 3.27% (4 x constepinf of the published
  SW07 mode) and its change over the last four quarters. RE(t-1) itself ends 1.25 pp below the
  steady state and the baseline 2.03 pp below, so "near" is judged against those two.
- Shares: end-of-quarter discrete-choice shares (n_end) of the types in the population, history
  1966Q1-2022Q2 and forecast; corner = some type below 0.02 or above 0.98; flips = a type moving
  between below 0.1 and above 0.9 and back. Only switching variants have shares to assess (in
  learning and fixed-share modes n_end is a shadow share that the model does not use).
- Facility events: app1_events.csv (history and forecast), and the Kalman history (rmse_runs).
- IRF nonlinearity: max |GIRF(+1 sd) + GIRF(-1 sd)| / peak |GIRF(+1 sd)| from irf_runs.csv, plus
  two shape checks from irf_long.csv: the largest peak relative to the RE(t-1) peak for the same
  shock and variable, and the number of shock-variable responses still above 90% of their peak
  at h = 19. irf_check.csv is a different check: the IRF method rerun as full hetlearn.run calls
  for 10 variant-shock pairs, all max differences 0 (method reproducible).
- Fit: one-step RMSE relative to the baseline and log likelihood minus the baseline's (-1839.8),
  Kalman history 1966Q1-2026Q2, plus the model gap (max |ALM forecast - agents' recorded
  forecast|).

Implementation judgement: (i) is there a validation that does not reuse the code under test (a
closed form, a hand-written reference model, a reproduction of a published result), or only a
neutral-value collapse and hand loops of the design's own recursion; (ii) faithfulness to the
source in scope, timing and parameters; (iii) model gap or unsourced (proposed, calibrated by us,
or converted) parameters.

Confidence score:
  5  independent validation, source-faithful, sourced parameters, clean behaviour, fit no worse
     than the baseline.
  4  as 5 with one disclosed scope or timing compromise, or a modest loss of fit.
  3  mechanism validated, but a material compromise (parameters transplanted from a re-estimated
     model, an unverified or rescaled parameter, an inert mechanism) or a behaviour flag short of
     instability. Recommendation: fix.
  2  behaviour fails (blow-up, corners, facility events, cycles) or the variant collapses onto
     another one, or its key parameter is unsourced; the machinery itself may be correct.
     Recommendation: drop.
  1  behaviour fails and the calibration or the mechanism's mapping cannot be relied on either.
     Recommendation: drop.
Score 4-5 = keep, 3 = fix, 1-2 = drop.

3. VARIANT VERDICTS
-------------------
References. RE(t-1): pinf 6.3 -> 1.7 (2024Q4) -> 2.0, one turning point, robs 2.7-4.9. Baseline
(BL): pinf 8.9 -> 0.9 (2025Q3) -> 1.2, robs to 7.1, shares 0.28-0.72, no events.

1a Level-k on a learned level 0 (EGM). Score 2, drop.
  Behaviour. One boom-bust cycle of 25 pp: pinf 11.7 (2022Q3), 17.7 (2023Q4), -7.9 (2026Q2) and
  still falling 8.4 pp over the last year; robs 1.0-15.6, dy -6.2 to 12.5, dc -6.2 to 9.1.
  Facility event projection_level at the origin 2022Q2 and in 2022Q3-2023Q2: with the learners'
  2022Q2 beliefs (beta 1.061) the level-1 forecast loads 1.168 on pinf(t-1) and the level economy
  has no stable solution, so beliefs revert and stale levels stay in force. From the calm 2019Q4
  state the IRFs are tame (asymmetry 0.015) and the in-sample fit matches the baseline (dLL +4.5,
  one facility event). EGM's level-k reasoning pulls expectations toward RE; here it amplifies,
  because the level-0 seed is explosive in exactly the quarter Application 1 starts from.
  Implementation. Best-validated machinery in the portfolio: A1 fixed-point gate (1e-14), A2 EGM
  closed form on a scalar economy (8.9e-16), A3 K = 0, A4 level-k on RE = RE, the design
  prototype's T-map (1.3e-14), K8 model gap 1e-14. Compromises: EGM's replicator dynamics replaced
  by the logit (chi 2, memory 0.8, no translation), equal initial shares proposed; the E-stability
  record (T-map Jacobian) promised in the design was not built.

1b Level 2 for everyone (BCL). Score 2, drop.
  Behaviour. pinf 13.1 -> 20.1 (2023Q3) -> -5.7; robs 0.4-16.0; dy -7.9 to 11.2; the same five
  facility events as 1a. dLL -75.
  Implementation. As 1a. BCL's level 2 is the wage setters' inflation nowcast; here it replaces
  every inflation expectation.

4 Shallow reasoning (Angeletos-Sastry; GHL values). Score 2, drop.
  Behaviour. No oscillation or event, but inflation stays at 9.3-9.6% for four quarters and is
  still falling 1.9 pp/yr at 2026Q2 (2.9%); robs peaks at 11.7% (BL 7.1, data 5.3). Monetary
  policy barely moves inflation (em -> pinf peak -0.006 against -0.189 under RE) and the output
  response on impact is 60% smaller (-0.30 against -0.77). Fit collapses: dLL -1960, RMSE dc 2.1x, dy 1.56x, robs 1.42x.
  Implementation. H2: the generated model equals a hand-written GHL model with four equation
  copies (RE limit); C1 equal copies = one multiplier. The weak point is the mapping: GHL's three
  lambdas come from a three-equation model and were assigned to SW07 equations by agent role by
  us (flagged in the design); lambda_pi 0.08 almost removes expected inflation from the Euler
  and Q equations; SW07's structural parameters stay at their RE estimates. There is no SW07-scale
  estimate to replace the mapping with.

5a Cognitive discounting, all expectations, m = 0.62. Score 3, fix.
  Behaviour. Application 1 path is clean: pinf 7.3 -> 3.5 with no turning point, 0.2 above steady
  state at 2026Q2; robs 2.9-6.7; dy 2.1-2.7; shares 0.36-0.64. Transmission almost disappears:
  em -> pinf peak -0.001 (RE -0.189), em -> dy impact -0.22 (RE -0.77). In-sample fit is the worst
  of the portfolio: dLL -4053, RMSE dc 2.4x, dy 1.77x, robs 1.52x.
  Implementation. H1: generated = hand-written CD model (p3_v02, m 0.62), neutral m = 1 = baseline,
  R4. The Gabaix closed-form check on the toy model listed in the design was not built (not in
  test_transforms). Scope and m follow Meggiorini's behavioral SW07, but Meggiorini estimated m
  together with every other SW07 parameter; here m sits on the published RE mode, and the
  likelihood shows the two do not fit together.

5b Cognitive discounting, learning set, m = 0.94. Score 4, keep.
  Behaviour. pinf 8.7 -> 2.2, one turning point, flat at the end (1.05 below steady state; RE
  1.25 below); robs 3.3-6.9; shares 0.35-0.65; no events; IRFs decay; em -> pinf peak -0.071 (BL
  -0.109), the damping CD predicts. dLL +130, pinf RMSE 0.905x, other RMSEs within 2.3%.
  Implementation. Same multiplier code as 5a (hand-written CD reference; neutral checks,
  including K7, a multiplier of 1 on every type's inflation forecast in the Kalman history). No non-neutral
  reference exists for the multiplier inside the learners' aggregate, but the transform there is
  one factor m^2 per type forecast, and the same transformed forecast enters fitness. m comes
  from Meggiorini-Milani's own model (one M with t-1 information, which matches the timing here),
  not from SW07.

6 As-if hyperbolic discounting, alpha = 0.25. Score 2, drop.

10 Diagnostic expectations, theta = 0.44. Score 4, keep.
  Behaviour. pinf 9.4 -> 2.5, flat at the end (0.8 below steady state); robs to 8.1; shares

17 Four heuristics (Anufriev-Hommes). Score 3, fix.
  Behaviour. Smooth: pinf 9.4 -> 1.7 (still falling 1.4 pp/yr), robs to 8.7, dy 0.3-4.6;
  epinf -> pinf builds from 0.54 on impact to 0.82 at h = 2 and undershoots by h = 19, the
  trend-following pattern. But the switching does nothing: all four shares stay within 0.16-0.33
  over 1966-2026 and 0.22-0.27 in the forecast. AH's intensity 0.4 is calibrated on squared
  errors in lab price units (prices near 60); SW07 inflation errors in % per quarter are a few
  tenths, so the squared-error differences are orders of magnitude smaller and the logit cannot
  separate the rules. The variant behaves as a fixed equal-weight average, which is not AH's
  mechanism (coordination on one rule, path dependence). In the Kalman history the model gap is
  0.90 (STR): the ALM's x{-2} and self{-1} terms use revised lags while the agents score real-time
  ones, so its dLL -241 and pinf RMSE 1.12x are not the variant's own.
  Implementation. The mechanism is reproduced: A1-A3 the engine equals an independent loop of the
  AH equations on AH's asset-pricing economy (prices 1.9e-13, shares 5.3e-15) and gives the three
  published patterns (monotone convergence, damped and persistent oscillation); A4 identical
  rules keep 0.25; L15 the decreasing-gain LAA anchor. The problem is the unit translation.

18 Targeters/extrapolators and optimists/pessimists (De Grauwe). Score 1, drop.
  Behaviour. dy and dc have five turning points each in 16 quarters (three reversals of 0.5 pp or
  more); dc swings -1.1 to 4.2. IRFs are explosive cycles: eb -> dc peaks 2.4, -5.6, 6.4, -8.9,
  10.4, -10.5 at a period of about 7 quarters; 12 of 28 responses are at their peak at h = 19;
  asymmetry 0.56 and peak 5.2x RE, both the largest in the portfolio. dLL -1015, dc RMSE 1.98x.
  Implementation. The parameters were picked by an outcome screen (app1_calib18.csv): 49 settings
  run and judged on the forecast itself (inflation and consumption inside data ranges, no corner
  shares); 4 non-design settings pass; the chosen g 0.25 and intensity 0.5 are half the converted
  published values. The design calibration (g 2.16, chi 2, memory 0.8) gives -17.9% average
  inflation. Checks V1 and A8 test only the bias arithmetic, not the switching economy. The c
  expectation stands in for the output-gap expectation SW07 does not have; g is unsourced.

19 Target believers (Hommes-Lustenhouwer). Score 4, keep.
  Behaviour. pinf 8.2 -> 2.0 -> 2.2 (1.1 below steady state); robs 3.2-6.0; forecast shares
  0.13-0.55; TB touched a corner in 6 quarters of 1974-81 (min 0.010). em -> pinf peak -0.085 (BL

  history the RE share falls to 0.009 by 2019Q4 (TB crowds out RE there, not in Application 1).
  Implementation. H2: a TB-only population = SW07 with every pinf{+1} replaced by 0, written by
  hand (2.1e-13); H1 TB at share 0 = baseline; K7 in the Kalman history. Compromises: {RE, BR, TB}
  is the review's nesting, not HL's {TB, naive} pair; intensity, memory and the 1/3 initial
  shares are the baseline's or proposed.

20 SAC learning on seven expectations (HMOZ). Score 3, fix.
  Behaviour. Application 1 path is smooth: pinf 8.9 -> 2.4 with no turning point (still falling
  1.8 pp/yr), robs to 10.3, dy 2.2-2.7, dc 1.4-2.1. The IRFs raise a flag: 7 of 28 responses are
  still rising at h = 19 (ew -> robs climbs linearly to 0.24), em -> dy on impact is -0.10 (RE
  -0.77), epinf -> robs peaks at 3.3x RE. Learned autocorrelations reach 0.99 (c, at 2022Q2;
  never clipped). Fit: dLL -1852, yet point RMSEs improve (dy 0.83x, labobs 0.82x, pinf 0.93x),
  so the predictive variance is badly scaled.
  Implementation. L1 batch identity with HMOZ's finite-sample terms (the engine runs the form
  without them; gap 0.039 at t = 10, 0.0055 at t = 400), L2 hand loop, L3 and L9 the [-1, 1]
  bound, L8 toy engine = hand loop. Faithfulness gaps: the gain 0.006 was read off HMOZ Table 1 by
  column order ("presumably SAC, MSV, VAR(1), AR(2)"), not confirmed; HMOZ re-estimate the
  structural parameters under SAC (investment adjustment cost 1.38 against 5.68 under RE, higher
  indexation), while this run keeps SW07's RE mode; initial beliefs from RE moments, not from
  BLE-simulated data.

5. CROSS-CUTTING FINDINGS
-------------------------
- Facility 'alm' keeps every run going but does not keep paths reasonable. Each variant with a
  facility event in Application 1 (1a, 1b, 25a, 25b, 27) also has the worst paths; under 'none'
  1a, 1b, 25a and 27 stopped in phases P2-P5. Treat any facility event as a disqualifier for
  the portfolio.
- Behaviour failures cluster where a learned PLM is explosive in 2022 (baseline AR(1) beta 1.061
  at the origin): 1a, 1b and 27 build on that learner; 25a and 25b have their own roots above 1.
- Parameters transplanted from re-estimated models: 4, 5a, 6, 20, 25a, 25b (and mildly 10) take
  behavioural parameters estimated jointly with other structural values and put them on SW07's RE
  mode. The dLL of -1700 to -4050 for 4, 5a, 6, 20, 25a and 25b is the symptom.
- Kalman model gap above rounding for 17 (0.90), 25a (12.5), 25b (5.3) and 33 (5.6): their
  in-sample RMSE and log likelihood use revised lags in the ALM, not the agents' forecasts.
  Application 1 is unaffected (the forecast stage asserts each type's forecast to 1e-9).
- Validations listed in the design but not found in the suites: the Gabaix closed form on the
  toy (5), "five identical models = one model" (25b), the T-map Jacobian record (1).
- The hand-written references (H1-H7) check the transform text at the RE limit with period-t RE
  information. The switching economy with transformed fitness is covered by neutral-value
  collapses and R4, not by a non-neutral reference.
- rmse_runs.csv, columns shares_2019Q4 and shares_2026Q2: for learning and fixed-share modes the
  strings print the shadow discrete-choice shares (br01b shows L0 0.503 / L2 0.497 under fixed
  L2 = 1; br20 shows pk RE 0.155 under 'learning'). Neither report uses those columns, but a
  reader of the CSV would be misled.
- RE(t-1) ends 1.25 pp below the inflation steady state at 2026Q2, so "back near steady state" is
  not a pass/fail test on its own; the kept variants end between 0.8 and 1.6 pp below, except 7
  (3.0 below, still flattening).
